# PRD Review Checklist

Use when a change has PRD or requirement artifacts.

- Requirement ID and TAPD ID are present.
- Goals and non-goals are explicit.
- Acceptance criteria are testable.
- Permissions and data isolation are stated for user-facing or admin-facing changes.
- Data metrics and reporting impact are stated for data changes.
- Rollout, rollback, and release criteria exist for core changes.
- Unknowns are preserved and routed to product or business owner.
